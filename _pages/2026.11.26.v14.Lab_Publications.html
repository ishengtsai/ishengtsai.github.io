iScience · 2026

Bringing wild yeast diversity into experimental genetics

We established a haploid collection from 32 Taiwanese Saccharomyces cerevisiae isolates. This resource enables experiments on reproductive isolation and ecological trait variation across genetically diverse wild lineages.我們以 32 株臺灣釀酒酵母菌（Saccharomyces cerevisiae）分離株建立單倍體菌株資源，讓研究者得以在遺傳背景多樣的野生譜系中，進行生殖隔離與生態性狀變異的實驗。

Environmental Microbiome · 2025

Fungal communities change across forest habitats and through time

Our study of tropical forests revealed high fungal community turnover and contrasting assembly processes across neighbouring habitats, linking spatial and temporal patterns to questions about how communities form.我們在熱帶森林的研究發現，真菌群落具有高度更替，相鄰棲地的群落組成過程也有所不同。這些結果將時空變化與群落形成的問題連結起來。

Nature Microbiology · 2024

A fungal partner promotes prey digestion in sundews

We identified the acidophilic fungus Acrodontium crateriforme as a microbial partner of sundews. Experiments with Drosera spatulata showed that the fungus promotes prey digestion, establishing a tractable system for studying plant–fungus interactions.我們鑑定出嗜酸性真菌 Acrodontium crateriforme 是毛氈苔的微生物夥伴。以 Drosera spatulata 進行的實驗顯示，此真菌能促進獵物消化，建立了可用於研究植物與真菌交互作用的實驗系統。
