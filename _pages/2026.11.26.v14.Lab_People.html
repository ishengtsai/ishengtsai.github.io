Principal investigator

Isheng Jason Tsai 蔡怡陞

Research Fellow · Biodiversity Research Center, Academia Sinica中央研究院生物多樣性研究中心・研究員

I study fungal and yeast ecology and evolution, and the interactions between microbes and their hosts. Our lab combines field sampling, experiments and genomic analyses to connect genome variation with biological diversity.我研究真菌與酵母菌的生態及演化，以及微生物與宿主之間的交互作用。實驗室結合野外採樣、實驗與基因體分析，探討基因體變異與生物多樣性的關聯。
