Research

We connect genetic variation, ecological diversity and the interactions between organisms.

Our research combines field sampling, experiments and genomic analyses to study fungal and yeast ecology and evolution, forest fungal communities and microbial interactions.

Genomes · Populations · Communities

Fungal/Yeast ecology and evolution

How do genetic variation and ecological conditions shape fungal diversity?遺傳變異與生態條件如何形塑真菌多樣性？

Wild yeast ecology and evolution

We investigate the diversity, population structure and ecological differentiation of wild yeasts. By combining population genomics with experimental genetics, we examine how genome variation relates to traits and reproductive isolation.

Our work connects sampling in natural environments with experiments on genetically diverse strains, drawing on Taiwan’s wild yeast diversity and collaborations across Asia and beyond.

We established a haploid collection from 32 Taiwanese Saccharomyces cerevisiae isolates to support experiments on reproductive isolation and ecological trait variation.

我們研究野生酵母菌的多樣性、族群結構與生態分化。透過結合族群基因體學與實驗遺傳學，我們探討基因體變異與性狀及生殖隔離之間的關聯。

我們以臺灣野生酵母菌的多樣性及跨亞洲等地區的合作為基礎，連結自然環境採樣與遺傳背景多樣菌株的實驗。

我們以 32 株臺灣釀酒酵母菌（Saccharomyces cerevisiae）分離株建立單倍體菌株資源，用於生殖隔離與生態性狀變異的實驗。

Habitats and communities

Forest fungal communities

At the community level, we study how forest fungal assemblages differ among habitats and change through time. Our work also examines the distribution of yeasts across microbiomes and how sampling and analytical methods influence the diversity we detect.

Our study of tropical forests revealed high fungal community turnover and contrasting assembly processes across neighbouring habitats. These observations help us investigate how fungal communities form and change.

在群落層次，我們研究森林真菌群落在不同棲地之間的差異及其隨時間的變化。我們也探討酵母菌在不同微生物群中的分布，以及採樣與分析方法如何影響所偵測到的多樣性。

我們在熱帶森林的研究發現，真菌群落具有高度更替，相鄰棲地的群落組成過程也有所不同。這些觀察有助於我們探討真菌群落如何形成與改變。

Plants · Microbes · Function

Microbial interactions

How do interactions among microbes and their hosts influence biological function?微生物彼此之間及其與宿主的交互作用，如何影響生物功能？

Carnivorous plants and their microbial partners

Carnivorous plants provide an experimental system for studying interactions among plants, microbes and insect prey. We combine microbial isolation, experiments and genomic analyses to investigate how microbial partners contribute to prey digestion and nutrient acquisition.

We also investigate how interactions among microbial community members affect these processes, distinguishing their occurrence alongside a host from their functional contributions.

Experiments with Drosera spatulata showed that the acidophilic fungus Acrodontium crateriforme promotes prey digestion, establishing a tractable system for studying plant–fungus interactions.

食蟲植物提供了研究植物、微生物與昆蟲獵物之間交互作用的實驗系統。我們結合微生物分離、實驗與基因體分析，探討微生物夥伴在獵物消化及養分獲取中的功能。

我們也研究微生物群落成員之間的交互作用如何影響這些過程，並區分微生物與宿主共同出現的現象及其實際功能。

以 Drosera spatulata 進行的實驗顯示，嗜酸性真菌 Acrodontium crateriforme 能促進獵物消化，建立了可用於研究植物與真菌交互作用的實驗系統。
