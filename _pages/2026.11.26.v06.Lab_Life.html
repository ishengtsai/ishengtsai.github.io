23 January 20252025 年 1 月 23 日

Getting ready for the Lunar New Year

We cleaned the lab before the end of the lunar year, continuing our annual tradition of preparing the shared workspace for the year ahead.我們在農曆年結束前整理實驗室，延續每年的大掃除傳統，讓共同使用的空間準備好迎接新的一年。

January 20252025 年 1 月

Sharing our work at MiTalk9

We attended MiTalk9 at Soochow University, organised by former lab member Huei-Mien. Yu-Chen presented a poster and received the second-place prize.我們參加了由前實驗室成員 Huei-Mien 在東吳大學舉辦的 MiTalk9。Yu-Chen 發表研究海報，並獲得第二名。

A potluck with our neighbours

We joined John Wang’s neighbouring lab for our year-end potluck and gift exchange. The gathering also included a game of Dixit.我們與隔壁 John Wang 的實驗室一起舉辦年末共餐及交換禮物，也玩了 Dixit 桌遊。

August 20242024 年 8 月

A summer outing near Yangmingshan

The lab welcomed summer interns Yo-C and En-Chen, mentored by Hung-Wei and Yu-Chen, and Audrey, who joined the TIGP Biodiversity programme. We marked the summer with khòng-iô (焢窯), cooking in an earth oven near Yangmingshan National Park.實驗室迎來由 Hung-Wei 與 Yu-Chen 指導的暑期實習生 Yo-C 和 En-Chen，以及加入 TIGP 生物多樣性學程的 Audrey。我們到陽明山國家公園附近焢窯，一起度過夏日時光。
